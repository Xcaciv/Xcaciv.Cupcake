## 1. [CRITICAL] §11.2 register header (S11-open-questions.md:38) and §1 Executive Summary (S01-executive-summary.md:61)

**Problem:** The claim "Fifty distinct quirks were catalogued" (repeated in §1 as "**50 are catalogued** (§11.2)") is materially wrong. Grepping "QUIRK" across §6 and all eleven §7 subsection files yields, after merging near-duplicates and consequence-of chains, at least 81 distinct quirks, each separately QUIRK-tagged with its own observable consequence and its own keep-or-fix decision. The register covers 50 of them; 31 are documented in the body and absent from the table. Because §10 Phase 0's deliverable is "a signed-off disposition for every entry in the §11.2 quirk register", every omitted row is a decision the clone team will never be asked to make — including four that make the shipped artefact non-functional and three that are supply-chain relevant. §7.9 FR-9.31 even flags its own omission in the body text: "QUIRK Q14 (newly identified in this subsection; not in the shared quirk register)".

**Required fix:** Change §11.2's opening sentence to "Eighty-one distinct quirks were catalogued." and §1's sentence to "**81 are catalogued** (§11.2)." Add the 31 rows given in the findings below (Q-51 … Q-81), and delete the parenthetical "(newly identified in this subsection; not in the shared quirk register)" from §7.9 FR-9.31 once Q-56 exists.
