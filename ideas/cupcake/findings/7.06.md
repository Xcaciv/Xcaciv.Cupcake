## 5. [MAJOR] §6 GR-41 (contradicted by §7.2 FR-2.17, §7.4 FR-4.8, §7.5 FR-5.17, §7.6 UC-6.4/AC-6.26, §7.10 FR-10.60, §11.2 Q-17)

**Problem:** GR-41 states the startup-failure rule as "Any failure of the three guarded startup steps **other than No Plugins Available** shall be replaced by a Startup Load Failure whose summary is the fixed, cause-independent literal `Unable to load commands.` — **identical in both Session entry points**". Read as written, the No-Plugins-Available exemption holds in both entry points. Six other locations say the opposite: the asynchronous/background entry point has no tolerant branch and wraps No Plugins Available as fatal too (`Loop.cs:82-85` has only the generic catch). §6 also never records the two unrelated "nothing found" signals or which one is fatal, although §7.6's external-technology note calls collapsing them "the single most consequential substitution error available here" and §7.5's calls the two distinguishable signals "critical". §6 is the product-wide normative section a clone team will build the failure contract from.

**Required fix:** Split the clause: "In the blocking entry point, any failure other than No Plugins Available is replaced by a Startup Load Failure …; in the background entry point there is no tolerant branch, so **every** failure including No Plugins Available is wrapped. The summary literal is identical in both." Add a new §6.5 rule stating that the Command Service raises two unrelated sibling conditions — "no verified Plugin Directory" (caught, becomes the guidance line) and "Plugin Directory holds no plugin binaries" (uncaught, fatal, exit 1) — and that collapsing them inverts the first-run experience, cross-referencing §7.4 FR-4.42 and §7.6 FR-6.32.

## 6. [MINOR] §11.1 / §11.2 Q-7, against §7.6 FR-6.49 and decision-list item 13 (assembled PRD lines 3748, 3852)

**Problem:** F9's open question 9 — "**Should registry-side diagnostics be surfaced?** Every registry call passes a discard sink, so a feed outage and an empty result look identical to the operator" — is documented in the body (FR-6.49, AC-6.25, §7.6 decision 13) but reaches neither §11.1 nor the §11.2 quirk register, which is the table Phase 0 signs off. It is not a resolved question: the disposition is still open. The concrete harm is that it interacts with a register entry that *is* there: §11.2 Q-7 recommends "**Fix** — emit an explicit 'no matches'" for zero-result searches. A team that implements Q-7 without knowing about FR-6.49 will print "No matches" when the registry is unreachable — turning a silent failure into an actively false statement.

**Required fix:** Add a row to the §11.2 quirk register: "Q-7a | Registry-side diagnostics are discarded at every call site, so a feed outage and an empty result are indistinguishable. | 7.6, 7.7 | U | **Fix** — surface registry diagnostics, or Q-7's 'no matches' message will lie during an outage." and add "see Q-7a" to Q-7's disposition cell so the two decisions are taken together.
